Questions roofers ask us

Don't see yours? Ask on a strategy call and we'll give you a straight answer.

Book a strategy call
Do you only work with roofing contractors?

Yes. Everything we build, from ad campaigns to follow-up scripts, is made for how roofing jobs are actually sold: inspections, insurance claims, estimates, and storm seasons.

How soon will I see new leads?

Paid campaigns can start producing calls within the first few weeks. Local search and reviews build over a few months. On your strategy call we'll give you a realistic timeline for your market.

Is ad spend included in the plan price?

No. Ad spend goes directly to the ad platforms on your own accounts, so you always own the data and can see exactly where every dollar went.

Will you work with my competitors?

We limit how many contractors we take on in each service area so we're never bidding against ourselves. Ask about availability in your market when you book a call.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

Terms depend on the plan. We'll walk through them on your strategy call, before you commit to anything.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to your existing accounts, photos of recent jobs, and someone on your team who can answer booked leads. We handle the rest.

Ready to keep your crews booked?

Tell us a little about your business. We'll come to the call with a look at your market and where your next signed jobs can come from.
